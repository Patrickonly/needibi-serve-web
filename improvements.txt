Needibi Serve Comprehensive Improvements, Features & Functional Specifications Needibi Serve should be a trusted, request-driven platform that helps people get products, services, vehicles, properties, professional talent, deals, and customized solutions served through a controlled process. Core tagline: Needibi Serve Get Your Needs Served. 1. User roles and permissions Needer Creates Needs and Bundles, discovers approved Deals, receives and evaluates offers, chooses providers, follows approved partners, saves items, and tracks fulfillment. Partner A verified or appropriately approved provider who serves Needs, submits offers, publishes Deals subject to approval, fulfills agreements, and builds a reputation. Agent Helps Needers submit requests and helps Partners onboard or participate. Agents access only assigned cases and can earn eligible, verified rewards. Admin Manages approvals, verification, matching, disputes, users, platform settings, payments or payment records, moderation, subscriptions, analytics, and operational quality. Recommendation: Remove the separate Contributor feature if it duplicates the Partner or Agent roles. Keep each role distinct, with permissions based on what it needs to do. 2. Core content types: Needs, Deals, and Bundles Needs What people want A Needer describes what they want to obtain or accomplish. Requests may cover products, services, vehicles, properties, professional talent, or customized solutions. Support text, images, video, and voice notes. Deals What is offered Approved offers from Needibi Serve or approved Partners. A Deal may represent a product, service, vehicle, property, professional service, custom solution, or a combination of items. Bundles Multiple Needs grouped together A Needer combines multiple Needs into one Bundle, such as furnishing a home or organizing an event. Bundles can be fulfilled as a whole or through individual Needs, subject to the Needers choices and the agreed terms. Approval policy Partner-submitted Deals must be reviewed and approved by Needibi Serve before public publication. Needs and Bundles should follow the appropriate moderation and publication rules. Reposted content must pass eligibility checks and any applicable moderation requirements. Only approved and currently published content should appear in public discovery. Clearly distinguish platform approval from a guarantee of a providers quality or future performance. 3. Card buttons and actions Needs cards Recommended actions: Serve A Partner indicates willingness to serve the Need and submits an offer, or begins the applicable serving workflow. Re-need Republishes substantially the same Need as a new record after confirmation and eligibility checks. Message Opens the moderated public message/comment workflow. Re-request Opens a prefilled request form based on the existing Need, allowing the Needer to customize and submit a new request. Save Adds the Need to saved items. Share Shares a canonical link to the Need, subject to privacy and access rules. Love Records a positive reaction, if reactions are enabled. View Details Opens the full Need page. More Options Provides actions such as reporting, copying the link, or other authorized actions. Bundles cards Recommended actions: Serve Re-need Message Re-request Save Share Love View Details More Options Bundle actions must make it clear whether an operation applies to the entire Bundle or an individual Need within it. Deals cards Recommended actions: Get Deal, Book, or Request Choose a label appropriate to the Deal type. Message Save Share Love View Details More Options Follow Partner Available when the Deal belongs to an approved Partner. Clearly distinguish Re-need from Re-request This distinction is essential for both the user experience and backend behavior. Re-need Purpose: Publish the same Need or Bundle again. 1. The Needer clicks Re-need. 2. A confirmation appears: Youre about to publish this Need again. Continue? 3. The system checks account status, rate limits, prohibited content, and applicable publication rules. 4. If eligible, the system creates a new record and publishes it immediately where policy permits; otherwise, it sends it for the required review. 5. The original record remains in history. Similar to reposting on a social platform. Re-request Purpose: Create a new, customizable request based on an existing Need or Bundle. 1. The Needer clicks Re-request. 2. A new request form opens, prefilled with relevant details from the existing item. 3. The Needer can change quantities, specifications, budget, location, timing, recurrence, and other fields. 4. The Needer reviews and submits the new request. 5. The new request follows the normal publication, moderation, and matching workflow. Similar to ordering again but customizing the new request before submission. Terminology rule: Use Re-request everywhere in the UI, documentation, notifications, analytics, and development specifications. Do not use Re-request Similar. 5. Moderated public Messages The Message feature should allow constructive public communication without turning Needibi Serve into an unrestricted contact-sharing platform. User workflow 1. The user clicks Message on a Need, Bundle, or Deal. 2. A prompt appears: Give your honest comment or message on this Need/Bundle/Deal. 3. The user submits the message. 4. The message appears in the senders own history as Pending Review. 5. An admin or specifically authorized verified Partner reviews it. 6. If approved, it becomes publicly visible. If rejected, the sender receives the reason and can edit and resubmit where permitted. Moderation controls Detect and restrict phone numbers, email addresses, URLs, social media handles, and attempts to share contact details. Detect variations such as spaced-out, punctuated, or spelled-out contact information. Apply spam, harassment, abuse, duplicate-message, and fraud checks. Provide a report action and moderation history. Use layered detection rather than relying on one keyword filter. Keep public moderated messages separate from private transaction chat. Partner matching algorithms Admins should be able to select the matching mode for the platform or configure it by request type. Mode A Manual matching Admins or authorized staff assign Partners to: Individual Needs. Entire Bundles. Selected Needs within a Bundle. This is useful for early operations, unusual requests, sensitive cases, and situations requiring human judgment. Mode B Automatic matching The system ranks eligible Partners using criteria such as: Relevant category, capability, and specifications. Verification and approval status. Service area and location. Availability, capacity, and recurring-service capability. Ratings and verified reviews. Fulfillment reliability and historical performance. Responsiveness and expected delivery time. Relevant experience and previous successful service. Important: Verification, eligibility, and authorization must be hard requirements where applicable, not merely scoring factors that a high rating can override. Mode C Limited-offer matching Limit how many active offers a Needer receives for each matching scope. Recommended workflow: 1. An eligible Partner clicks Serve and submits an offer. 2. The system allocates an available offer slot atomically. 3. The Needer can accept, reject, or request clarification through the permitted workflow. 4. Rejected, expired, or withdrawn offers release their slots. 5. The system continues matching eligible Partners until the limit is reached. 6. If the current round is rejected, matching can restart with eligible Partners who have not already been exhausted. 7. When all slots are occupied, additional Partners see that offers are full and can opt to receive a notification when a slot becomes available. 8. Admins are alerted when rejections reach the configured threshold. Recommended starting limit: Three active offers per individual Need at a time. Track both the number of distinct provider offers rejected by the Needer and the number of fully rejected matching rounds. Re-request: Start a new, customizable request based on an existing Need or Bundle. Serve: Submit an offer or begin the applicable service workflow. Message: Submit a public message subject to review. Private chat: Communicate within an authorized transaction. These definitions should remain consistent across the interface, backend logic, user manual, notifications, and analytics.
